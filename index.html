Which Country Uses the +49 Calling Code?

The international telephone calling code +49 belongs to Germany. It is assigned to the German telephone numbering system and is used when making international calls to German phone numbers.

Germany is located in Central Europe and is known for its major cities, international businesses, technology, and strong communication infrastructure. The +49 prefix allows international telephone networks to route calls toward German destinations.

What Does the +49 Country Code Mean?

The plus sign (+) represents the international dialing prefix. The numbers following it identify the destination country. In the case of Germany, the country code is 49.

For example, a phone number beginning with +49 indicates that it belongs to the German numbering system. The remaining digits identify the relevant mobile network, geographic area, or individual subscriber number.

Difference Between +49 and 0049

Both +49 and 0049 represent Germany's international calling code. The difference is in the way the international dialing prefix is written.

The plus sign is a universal notation that allows mobile devices to use the appropriate international access prefix. In many European countries, including Germany, 00 is commonly used as the international dialing prefix when entering numbers manually.
